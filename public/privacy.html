Privacy Policy

Lifted Farms operations app · Last updated October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the Lifted Farms operations app (the "App") handles, how we use it, and the choices you have. The App is operated by DNA Organics, Inc. (dba Lifted Farms and Lifted Organics), a California company ("we", "us").

Who uses the App

The App is a private business tool. It is used by our employees and contractors to run our farming, processing, distribution, sales and accounting work, and by our business customers to view their own orders, invoices and statements. It is not offered to the general public and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Information we handle

  • Account and sign-in information: your name, email address and the details needed to sign you in (by an emailed code, a password, or your Microsoft work account), and the access level you have in each part of the App.
  • Business records: customer and supplier business details (company and legal names, business licenses, billing and shipping addresses, billing contacts, phone numbers and email addresses), orders, invoices, payment terms, inventory, harvest, processing, nursery, shipping and compliance records.
  • Workforce records: for our staff and crews, work time, tasks, piece-rate and payroll records used to pay people and track labor costs.
  • Activity records: a log of important changes made in the App, which user made them and when.
  • QuickBooks Online data: described in the next section.

QuickBooks Online

An authorized accounting administrator can connect the App to our own QuickBooks Online company. The App connects only to our company. When connected, the App:

  • Creates our sales invoices in QuickBooks, and creates a QuickBooks customer when one does not already exist.
  • Reads our company name, and the names and IDs of our products and services, classes and payment terms, so invoice lines are coded the way our books expect.
  • Reads invoices it created, plus open invoices, open credit memos, unapplied payments and customer balances, so the App can show what has been paid and what is still owed.
  • Reads price changes made to those invoices in QuickBooks, so the App's copy matches.

We use QuickBooks data only to keep our own sales and accounts receivable records in step with our books. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside the service providers listed below. The QuickBooks sign-in tokens are kept on our server, in a database table that only the server can read. They are never shown in the App or sent to a browser.

An accounting administrator can disconnect QuickBooks at any time in the App (Accounting > Settings > Disconnect), which revokes the App's access with Intuit and deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be removed from within QuickBooks Online. Records already copied into the App, such as invoice balances and the matching QuickBooks IDs, stay part of our business records.

How we use information

  • To run our business: taking and filling orders, invoicing, collecting payment, paying our people, and managing inventory and operations.
  • To meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations, including required reporting and record keeping.
  • To keep the App secure, control who can see and change what, and troubleshoot problems.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers that host and run the App on our behalf, such as Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database and sign-in), Intuit (QuickBooks Online), Microsoft (work sign-in and document backup) and Resend (notification emails). They may use it only to provide their services to us.
  • With government agencies and regulators where the law requires it.
  • With the customer an order, invoice or statement is for, when we send or share it with them.
  • If required by law, or to protect our rights, our people or others.

Security

The App is served over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access requires signing in, and each user sees only the parts of the App their role allows. Customers see only their own records. Passwords, secret keys and sign-in tokens for connected services are kept on the server, not in the App's pages. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to the people who need it and remove access when it is no longer needed.

How long we keep information

We keep business, accounting and compliance records for as long as we need them to run the business and for the record-keeping periods the law requires, including for tax and employment records. Sign-in access is removed when someone no longer works with us.
