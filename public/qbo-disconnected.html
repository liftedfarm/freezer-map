QuickBooks is disconnected

The Lifted Farms app no longer has access to your QuickBooks Online company. Until it's reconnected:

  • Invoices won't be sent to QuickBooks. New invoices wait in Accounting > To send, and if auto-send is on they go once QuickBooks is reconnected.
  • Paid status and balances won't update from QuickBooks.

If you meant to disconnect, there's nothing else to do. To reconnect, an Accounting Admin signs in to the app and clicks Connect QuickBooks in Accounting > Settings.
